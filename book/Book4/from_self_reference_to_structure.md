
**FACT:** The transfer matrix T = [[0,1],[1,1]] encodes the carry rule as a linear map on a 2-component state space.

*"2-dimensional" = two independent parameters (algebraic), not two spatial directions (geometric).*

## Step 3: Observing the Sign Alternation

*Step 3 derives no new mathematics. It observes what already exists — the discrete sign alternation created by the negative eigenvalue — and names its properties. This observation is needed because the sign alternation creates the Z₂ symmetry and period-2 structure that distinguish even from odd positions throughout the framework.*

### What to observe

**FACT:** The backward eigenmode's contribution at position n is (−1/φ)ⁿ = (−1)ⁿ × (1/φ)ⁿ. This separates into:

- **(1/φ)ⁿ** — magnitude, always positive, always decaying
- **(−1)ⁿ** — sign, alternating: +1, −1, +1, −1, ...

The sign factor creates two classes of positions:

| Position n | (−1)ⁿ | Backward mode | Effect on total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0, 2, 4, ... (even) | +1 | Same sign as forward | Total enhanced: L(n) = φⁿ + (1/φ)ⁿ |

### The first nontrivial periodicity

**FACT:** The sign factor (−1)ⁿ has period 2 — the smallest possible period for an alternating sequence. This is the first structure in the framework that changes and returns. The forward mode grows without repeating.

### The Z₂ symmetry

**FACT:** The even/odd classification is a Z₂ symmetry — the simplest discrete symmetry group ({+1, −1} under multiplication). It was present from Step 1 (two roots, one positive, one negative). On the tower, it becomes a position-dependent pattern.

### The totals are Lucas numbers

**FACT:** The total content at each position is L(n) = φⁿ + (−1/φ)ⁿ, the Lucas number at position n: 2, 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, 18, 29, ...

**FACT:** The differences between adjacent totals satisfy L(n) − L(n+1) = −L(n−1) (from the Lucas recurrence). The differences are Lucas numbers — the pattern is self-similar.

## Step 4: The Hard-Core Constraint
