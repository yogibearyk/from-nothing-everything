# From Nothing, Everything

### What Happens When Wholeness Looks Inward

> *શૂન્ય માંથી બધું સર્જાયું*

---

A book exploring how the act of self-observation — wholeness looking inward — may force the fundamental constants of nature. Starting from an undifferentiated ground (Advait, non-duality) and following the logic of self-examination through ten axioms, the framework derives forty-four numerical predictions spanning coupling constants, cosmological parameters, quark masses, and neutrino mixing — all with zero adjustable parameters.

The book is written in two layers:

- **The Idea** — plain English, one metaphor per chapter, no equations. Read these sections alone for the complete story.
- **The Mathematics** — derivations in the framework's native notation, with decimal checks against experiment. This is where the numbers earn their keep.
